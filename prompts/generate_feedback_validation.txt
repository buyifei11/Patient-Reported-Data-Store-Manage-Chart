Write ten short, fictional English patient reports from the supplied fact seeds.
Return only the required report_id/text pairs. Keep every report independent.
Follow each seed's layout and style while preserving all and only its facts.
Use varied natural first-person phrasing, contractions, and words or digits for
numbers. Do not reuse one sentence template for every report.

Meaning constraints:
- A day_to_date count is the total from the start of today up to this submission.
  Say "so far today", "today up to now", or an equally explicit daily cumulative
  phrase. This also applies when the count is one, zero, or unknown.
- Anchor each reported symptom to today, the patient-local submission date.
- Each non-null severity is that symptom's WORST severity SO FAR TODAY on a fixed
  scale from 1 to 5. Express its score, scale, and context explicitly. When two
  symptoms occur in the same report, name the symptom in each rating clause.
- Zero vomiting means no vomiting so far today, with no vomiting severity score.
- Present vomiting with an unknown count means vomiting occurred today, but the
  cumulative number is unknown. Do not invent a number or a reason for uncertainty.
- Nausea has no episode count in these facts. Do not describe a nausea count.
- Do not mention a symptom absent from a seed. An unmentioned symptom is not an
  instruction to invent either its presence or its absence.
- Submission time is metadata, not a symptom occurrence time. Do not introduce
  occurrence clocks, other dates, completed-day totals, or narrower time windows.
- Do not add medications, causes, diagnoses, treatments, extra symptoms, severity
  adjectives, corrections, or explanations not present in the seed facts.

The layout instructions control where facts occur in the text. For interleaved
reports, retain the intervening other-symptom sentence or clause, and keep each
rating's symptom explicit. Do not discuss datasets, JSON fields, extraction,
evidence quotations, expected answers, or evaluation within the patient text.
