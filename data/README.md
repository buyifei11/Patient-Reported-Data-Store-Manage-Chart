# Dataset roles

All reports are fictional and written in English. These files are for extraction development and evaluation, not model training or clinical validation.

| Set | Sources | References | Reports | Reference observations | Targeted extraction checks |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original development | `raw/reports.jsonl` | `gold/expected.jsonl` | 10 | 15 | 3 |
| Additional development | `dev/reports.jsonl` | `dev/expected.jsonl` | 20 | 21 | 21 |
| Held-out comparison | `heldout/reports.jsonl` | `heldout/expected.jsonl` | 20 | 25 | 17 |
| Total | | | 50 | 61 | 41 |

Each JSONL line is one report, not one observation. A report may describe multiple symptoms or no target symptoms. `report_id` joins a report to its reference record. Original IDs are R001-R010, additional development IDs are D001-D020, and held-out IDs are H001-H020. Do not combine observation counts with report counts.

The prompt experiment uses the original and additional development sets together: 30 development reports, with 36 reference observations and 24 targeted checks. The held-out set remains separate. Running it twice does not turn 20 independent test cases into 40.

`cases.json` records each new report's scenario group, focus, and rationale. `review.md` records pre-inference review. Authors worked separately; labels were authored from the contract, not generated by the normalizer. A separate reviewer checked the held-out labels and cross-split narrative overlap, and two near-template narratives were rewritten before inference. These are agent-reviewed, AI-assisted synthetic references, not independent human clinical annotations.

Data generation is intentionally different from the later four-week follow-up dataset: most new reports are isolated edge cases. They do not establish longitudinal coverage or test complete correction-aware aggregation.

## Preserve the evaluation boundary

- Source text alone enters the LLM. Questionnaire answers and submission metadata are handled by code.
- Gold is used only for validation and scoring. It is never inserted into the extraction prompt.
- `observations` are final normalized references; they cannot directly train the text-only extractor. For example, a reconciled unknown count can originate from a clearly stated text count plus a conflicting questionnaire answer.
- `extraction_checks` inspect selected candidate fields before reconciliation. They are not a complete training dataset of candidate objects.
- No prompt or label changes are made in response to held-out outputs within the frozen experiment. Future tuning based on those errors turns these cases into known regression data; a later independent evaluation needs new cases.

See [the frozen experiment protocol](../docs/prompt_experiment.md) for versions, run counts, controls, and interpretation.
